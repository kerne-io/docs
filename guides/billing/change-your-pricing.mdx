---
title: 'Change your pricing'
description: 'Change a plan without surprising the customers already on it'
---

You're raising a price, shrinking an allowance, or moving a feature to a higher tier. The edit takes a minute. What deserves a decision is what happens to the customers who bought the old terms.

## Prices don't move on their own

A new price applies to new checkouts only. Stripe keeps charging existing subscribers what they signed up for, until they change plan. The same goes for a usage rate.

## What a plan includes: you choose

Edit the plan, click **Save**, and pick who gets the change:

| Save option                          | Who gets the change                                                              |
| ------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Update this offer**                | Everyone on this offer, today's subscribers included                             |
| **Start selling to new subscribers** | New buyers only. Everyone already subscribed keeps their offer, renewal included |

**Start selling to new subscribers** makes your edit the new current offer. The previous one stays, for the customers on it.

To check the result, call `allows()` on the feature you changed, for an existing subscriber and for a new one. After **Start selling to new subscribers**, their answers differ.

## Offers

Click **Offers**, next to the product's name, to see every version: the current offer, drafts, previous offers, and how many customers are on each. From there:

- **New offer** prepares the next version as a draft. Nothing is sold until you start selling it.
- **Sell this offer again** puts a previous offer back on sale.
- **Update rights on every offer** applies your unsaved edits to every version at once, including the ones existing customers kept.

## Moving customers forward

Two things move a customer onto newer terms, and both are deliberate:

- **They change plan.** They get the new plan's current offer and price.
- **You move them.** In **Offers**, under **Move customers**, pick the version to move from and the one to move to. It changes what they get, not what they pay. Customers move between versions of the same plan only.
