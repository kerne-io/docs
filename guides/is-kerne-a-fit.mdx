---

**title:** 'Is Kerne a good fit?'  
**description:** 'Where it works, and where it doesn’t'

---

Kerne is for products where access belongs to an individual customer.

If you sell to people (or one clear billing contact) and want to stop putting commercial rules in your code, it fits.  
If you need organizations, shared pools, and complex roles, it does not — yet.

### It fits when

- You sell to individual users or one person owns the billing
- You want `allows('export_pdf')` instead of `if (user.plan === 'pro')`
- You will change pricing and existing customers must keep what they bought
- You use Stripe and want payment and access cleanly separated
- You need real metering without building it yourself

**Strong fits:** per-seat SaaS, usage-based APIs, AI products, good-better-best plans.

### It does not fit (yet) when

- You need full organizations or teams with pooled allowances
- You need roles and resource-level permissions
- Social login is required at launch and you refuse to keep identity elsewhere
- You want to create products and prices only from code

### The simple test

If the question is _“Can this person do X right now?”_ → Kerne is the right layer.  
If the question is _“What can this company and these roles do under a shared pool?”_ → look elsewhere for now.
