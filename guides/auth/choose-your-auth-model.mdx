Your registration mode is one of the first decisions to make, controlled by a single `registration_mode` setting read from `GET /v1/auth/config` - see [Registration modes](/concepts/identity/registration-modes) for the exact logic.

## Recommendation

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Consumer / self-serve" icon="users">
    `registration_mode: 'OPEN'`. Friction is the enemy - every extra step loses signups.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Early access / high-touch onboarding" icon="building">
    `registration_mode: 'INVITE_ONLY'`, or a waitlist first. Fewer, more qualified signups, and room to onboard people by hand while you're still learning what they need.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Setting it

Registration mode is configured in the dashboard under **Settings → Authentication**.

## What Kerne doesn't have (yet)

- **No organizations/teams.** A subject is always one user - there's no "colleague joins the same workspace" concept. If your product bills a group as one unit (shared workspace, seats, team billing), that's not built yet - see [Subject: the concept that decides fit](/concepts/overview#subject-the-concept-that-decides-fit).
- **No SSO/MFA.** Email+password and magic link (see [Identity overview](/concepts/identity)) - nothing enterprise-auth yet.
- **No OAuth/social login.** Only credential-based auth.

If any of those are load-bearing for your launch, it's worth knowing now rather than after building against Kerne.
