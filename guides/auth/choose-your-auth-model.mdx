Your registration mode is one of the first decisions to make, and it's controlled by two flags read from `GET /v1/auth/config` - see [Registration modes](/concepts/identity/registration-modes) for the exact logic.

## Recommendation

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Consumer / self-serve" icon="users">
    Open registration (`allow_registration: true`, `require_invitation: false`). Friction is the enemy - every extra step loses signups.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Early access / high-touch onboarding" icon="building">
    Invite-only (`require_invitation: true`) or a waitlist first. Fewer, more qualified signups, and room to onboard people by hand while you're still learning what they need.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Setting it

Registration mode is tenant *configuration* - the rules your SaaS runs under - not something a running request should be able to flip. Kerne keeps that distinction sharp on purpose: you set it once, in the dashboard, and every runtime SDK call (`kerne.auth.signup()`, `kerne.auth.config()`) simply operates under whatever rule is currently in force. That's why there's no `kerne.settings.update()` next to `signup()` - mixing "define the rules" and "apply the rules" into the same surface is exactly what makes a SaaS's auth config accidentally mutable from application code.

## What Kerne doesn't have (yet) that might change your plan

Be honest with yourself about these before committing to a model - working around a missing feature is more expensive than knowing upfront it isn't there:

- **No organizations/teams.** Every scope is one user - there's no "colleague joins the same workspace" concept. If your product is inherently collaborative (shared workspace, seats, team billing), that's not built yet.
- **No SSO/MFA.** Email+password and a passwordless-start flow (see [Identity overview](/concepts/identity)) - nothing enterprise-auth yet.
- **No OAuth/social login.** Only credential-based auth.

If any of those are load-bearing for your launch, it's worth knowing now rather than after building against Kerne.
