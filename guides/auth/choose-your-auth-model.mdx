---
title: 'Choose your auth model'
description: 'Open, waitlist, or invite-only'
---

Who is allowed to create an account is one tenant-wide setting, `registration_mode` — see [Registration modes](/concepts/identity/registration-modes) for the exact rules, including the user limit that's checked before it.

The choice is a product one, not a technical one.

## Recommendation

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Consumer / self-serve" icon="users">
    `registration_mode: 'OPEN'`. Friction is the enemy — every extra step loses signups.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Early access / high-touch onboarding" icon="building">
    `registration_mode: 'INVITE_ONLY'`, or a waitlist first. Fewer, better-qualified signups, and
    room to onboard people by hand while you're still learning what they need.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

You can move between them later. Switching from invite-only to open doesn't invalidate the codes you already handed out.

## Setting it

In the dashboard, under **Settings → Authentication**.

<Note>
  Kerne's authentication is email + password and magic link. If you need SSO, MFA, or social login on
  day one, see [Is Kerne a fit?](/guides/is-kerne-a-fit#social-and-oauth-login) — you can keep your
  own identity provider and still use Kerne for access decisions.
</Note>
