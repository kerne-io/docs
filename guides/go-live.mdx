The dashboard shows the same checklist in your project. Four items block a launch; the rest make it look like yours.

## Before you launch

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect Stripe">
    **Settings → Payment provider**. No plan can carry a price without it. See [Connect your billing provider](/guides/getting-started/subscription-setup).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish your catalog">
    In **Catalog**: a product, its plans, and at least one feature. Check that every plan you sell has a price, and that **Default** includes what a customer should get before paying.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your production URL">
    **Settings → General → Production URL**. After checkout or sign-in, customers are sent back there. Set **Send users back to** if they should land on another page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Restrict allowed domains">
    **Settings → API keys → Allowed domains**. While the list is empty, any website can call Kerne with your app id. Add your app's domain; `*.example.com` covers its subdomains.

    Once the list has an entry, every other origin is refused, your local one included: add `localhost:3000` if you still develop against this project.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Also worth doing

- **Decide who can sign up.** New projects are invite only. Open sign-up under **Settings → Authentication → Who can sign up** if anyone should be able to create an account.
- **Add your branding.** A logo and a primary color, in **Settings → General**, make the hosted portal look like your product.
- **Turn on magic link** if you want sign-in without a password: **Settings → Authentication**.
- **Save Stripe's customer portal settings once**, in your Stripe dashboard. Stripe refuses to open its billing portal in live mode until you have.
- **Check where your secret key lives.** Server only, never in a browser bundle or a mobile app. If it ever leaked, rotate it in **Settings → API keys**: the old key stops working at once.

## Try it end to end

Before announcing anything, go through it once as a customer: sign up, subscribe to a plan, come back to your app, use a limited feature, open the billing portal, cancel. Then look at that customer under **Customers** in the dashboard.
