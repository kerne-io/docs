<Note>
There's no per-user permission system - no custom roles, no per-resource permissions, no admin flag on `User`. Admin access to your tenant is a property of the credential you authenticate with, not of the user.
</Note>

A regular user session (JWT, from `login()`/`signup()`) can only ever act on its own subject: its own profile, its own entitlements, its own usage. There is no way to elevate a user session to admin - not by setting a field, not by any client-side call.

## What requires your secret key

Routes gated `@RequireAdmin()` server-side accept **only** your secret key - called from your own backend, never from a client session. That covers things like:

- Listing/managing other users (`GET/PATCH /v1/users/:id` for someone other than yourself)
- Usage corrections (`kerne.admin.setUsage/adjustUsage/resetUsage`)
- Entitlement overrides (`kerne.admin.grant/revokeGrant`)
- Waitlist and invitation management

If you're building your own admin dashboard on top of Kerne, that dashboard's backend calls these routes with your secret key after applying your own access control (deciding which of your team members may reach that backend route in the first place) - Kerne has no opinion on who counts as an admin within your product.
