---
title: "Revoke invitation"
description: "Revoke an invitation"
---

Revoke an invitation token or code by its ID. It will no longer be usable. Requires your secret key, or a JWT owner crossing into their own tenant.

```typescript
await kerne.invitations.revoke('inv_123');
```

This works for both invitation tokens and join codes - `id` is the `id` field [`list()`](/sdks/server/invitations/list) returns for either one.
